I am Sidong Zhang, a Ph.D student in the [Information Fusion Lab](https://groups.cs.umass.edu/infofusion/) at College of Information and Computer Sciences, UMass Amherst, advised by [Prof. Madalina Fiterau](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~mfiterau/). I am the teaching assistant of graduate level CS 651 optimization class. 

My primary research interests are **Computer Vision**, **Multimodal Learning**, **information theory**, and **Deep Learning in Healthcare**. I am currently working on controllable image editing.

In my spare time, I love running, building gumpla models, and enjoying animes (this years goat goes to [chou kaguya hime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_Princess_Kaguya!)) :)
